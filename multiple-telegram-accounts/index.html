FAQ

Multiple Telegram accounts — questions

How many Telegram accounts can I use on one PC?

In GlotChat, as many as your plan includes — Starter 5, Pro 15, Business unlimited (per app, so your WhatsApp numbers don't use them up). Memory is the practical limit: about 300–450 MB per account.

How do I add a Telegram account?

Click Add account, choose Telegram, then scan the QR code with your phone (Telegram → Settings → Devices → Add device) or log in with your phone number.

Is it allowed by Telegram?

Yes. GlotChat opens the official Telegram Web for each account, exactly like logging in from a browser. Follow Telegram's rules — no spam or bulk unsolicited messages.

Can I use Telegram and WhatsApp together?

Yes. Both appear on the left of the same window; switch between apps and accounts with one click.
