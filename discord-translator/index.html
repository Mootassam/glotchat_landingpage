FAQ

Discord translator — questions

Can I auto-translate Discord messages?

Yes. In GlotChat every Discord message in the channel or DM you open is translated automatically and shown under the original.

Does it translate what I write?

Yes. Write in your language and press Enter: GlotChat shows the translation and what the other person will read before you send.

Can I use several Discord accounts at once?

Yes. Each Discord account runs in its own isolated session, side by side — as many as your plan includes.

Are voice chats translated?

No. GlotChat translates text messages on Discord. Voice notes are translated on WhatsApp and Telegram.

Is it the official Discord?

Yes. GlotChat opens the official Discord website for each account and never sends anything by itself.
