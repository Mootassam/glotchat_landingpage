How to use multiple Telegram accounts on PC

Telegram Desktop handles a few accounts one at a time. Here's how to run more — side by side — and how to translate them for customers abroad.

Sellers, support teams and agencies often need several Telegram accounts: a personal one, one per shop, one per market. Here are the ways to use them on a Windows computer.

Quick answer: for up to three accounts, add them in Telegram Desktop (Settings → Add account) and switch between them. For more accounts — or to see them side by side and translate chats — use Telegram Web in separate browser profiles, or a multi-account app like GlotChat.

What you need first

  • A phone number per Telegram account. You log in once with a code sent to that account, or by scanning a QR code from a phone where the account is already open (Telegram → Settings → Devices → Add device).
  • A Windows PC. Each open account in a multi-account app uses roughly 300–450 MB of memory.

Method 1: Telegram Desktop's account switcher (up to 3, free)

  1. Install Telegram Desktop and log in with your first account.
  2. Open the menu → Add account and log in with the next one.
  3. Switch from the menu. Notifications from all accounts arrive in the same app.

Good for: two or three accounts. Limits: only one account is shown at a time; free accounts are limited to three (Telegram Premium raises the limit); one proxy setting for everything; and nothing is translated automatically for you to reply in.

Method 2: Telegram Web in browser profiles (several, free)

  1. In Chrome or Edge, create a profile per account (profile picture → Add profile).
  2. In each profile, open web.telegram.org and log in.
  3. Pin the profiles to the taskbar.

Good for: a handful of accounts. Limits: one window per account, notifications that don't say which account they're for, and still no translation of your replies.

Method 3: A multi-account app (many accounts, one window)

GlotChat runs each Telegram account in its own isolated session, next to your WhatsApp numbers:

  1. Install GlotChat on Windows 10 or 11 and click Add account → Telegram.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone, or log in with your phone number.
  3. Name the account ("Shop RU", "Support") and pick your language. Optionally give it its own proxy.
  4. Every message is translated under the original. Write replies in your language — GlotChat shows what the customer will read, then sends it in theirs.

Good for: many accounts, WhatsApp and Telegram together, and customers who write in other languages.

Staying safe with several accounts

Using the official Telegram apps and Telegram Web is allowed. What gets accounts limited is behaviour: unsolicited bulk messages, adding strangers to groups, or spam reports. Talk to people who contacted you, keep messages relevant, and use a stable proxy per account if you manage accounts for different countries.

FAQ

Quick answers

How many Telegram accounts can I have on Telegram Desktop?

Up to three on a free account; Telegram Premium raises the limit. Only one is shown at a time.

Can I use more than 3 Telegram accounts on one PC?

Yes — with Telegram Web in separate browser profiles, or with a multi-account app like GlotChat that shows them side by side.

Can I translate Telegram chats on PC?

Telegram can translate single messages you select. GlotChat translates every message automatically and also translates your replies, with a check before sending.
