How to use WhatsApp on two phones at the same time

Whether you want the same WhatsApp on a second phone, two numbers on one phone, or all your numbers open at once on a PC — here are the ways that work in 2026, with the steps and the limits.

Updated 2026-11-06 · 3 min read

  2. 2. Two different WhatsApp numbers on one phone

There are three different things people mean by "WhatsApp on two phones", and each has its own answer. Figure out which one you need, then follow that section.

Quick answer: the same account on a second phone → link it as a companion device. Two numbers on one phone → use the built-in second-account feature (most Android phones). Many numbers at once → open them together on a PC with an app like GlotChat.

1. The same WhatsApp account on a second phone

WhatsApp lets one account run on your main phone plus up to four extra "linked" devices — and a second phone can be one of them.

  1. On the second phone, install WhatsApp and tap Use existing account / Link to existing account.
  2. On your main phone, open Settings → Linked devices → Link a device and scan the code.
  3. Both phones now show the same chats. The linked phone keeps working even when the main phone is off for a while.

Good for: reading and replying from a work phone and a personal phone. Limits: it's still one number, and each phone must come online now and then.

2. Two different WhatsApp numbers on one phone

Many Android phones can run two WhatsApp accounts at once.

  1. In WhatsApp, open Settings and tap the arrow next to your name, or your phone's Dual apps / Second space feature.
  2. Add the second number and verify it.
  3. Switch between the two accounts inside the app.

Good for: a personal and a business number on the same phone. Limits: only two accounts, and on iPhone you usually need the WhatsApp Business app for the second number.

3. Many WhatsApp numbers at once — on your PC

When you run a shop, a team or several countries, two is not enough — and phones are slow to switch between. A desktop app opens every number in one window. With GlotChat:

  1. Install GlotChat on Windows 10 or 11 and click Add account for each number.
  2. Scan each one's QR code (WhatsApp → Linked devices) and name it — "Shop UAE", "Sales — Ahmed".
  3. Switch with one click; every notification shows which account it's from. We've tested 27 accounts running at once.
  4. Turn on translation and every chat is translated both ways — handy if your customers write in other languages. Telegram accounts open in the same window too.

Will using two phones get my account banned?

No — linked devices and dual accounts are official WhatsApp features. What gets accounts banned is behaviour: spam, bulk unsolicited messages, and modified apps like "GB WhatsApp". Use your numbers normally and you're fine.

FAQ

Quick answers

Can I use the same WhatsApp account on two phones?

Yes. Link the second phone as a companion device from Settings → Linked devices on your main phone. Both phones then show the same chats.

Can I have two WhatsApp numbers on one phone?

On most Android phones, yes — add a second account in WhatsApp settings or your phone's dual-apps feature. On iPhone, use WhatsApp Business for the second number.

How do I run many WhatsApp numbers at once?

Open them together on a PC with a multi-account app like GlotChat, which has been tested with 27 accounts running at the same time, each in its own session.
