How to translate Telegram messages on a PC

Three ways to read and reply to Telegram chats in another language on Windows — copy-paste, a translation bot, or an app that translates both ways right in the chat — with the steps, the limits and the privacy trade-offs.

Updated 2026-11-06 · 3 min read

  2. Method 2: A Telegram translation bot (free, groups)

Telegram is used all over the world, so many chats cross a language line — a customer in one country, a supplier in another, a group where half the people write in a language you don't read. On a computer you have three ways to handle it.

Quick answer: for the odd message, copy-paste into an online translator. For a group you follow, a translation bot can help. To actually chat — read every incoming message in your language and have your replies translated before you send them — use an app like GlotChat that translates both ways inside Telegram.

Method 1: Copy-paste into an online translator (free, occasional)

  1. Select the message in Telegram and copy it.
  2. Paste it into Google Translate, DeepL or Bing Translator.
  3. Read the result; to reply, type your answer, translate it, and paste it back.

Good for: one message now and then. Limits: slow for a real conversation, nothing is translated automatically, and your text is sent to a third-party website.

Method 2: A Telegram translation bot (free, groups)

Some bots translate messages you forward to them, and a few can be added to a group to translate on request.

  1. Search Telegram for a translation bot and start it.
  2. Forward a message to the bot, or use its command in a group.
  3. Read the reply it sends back.

Good for: the occasional group message. Limits: you forward messages one by one, your own replies aren't translated for you, voice notes and photos aren't covered, and the bot's owner sees whatever you send it.

Method 3: An app that translates both ways in the chat (many languages, private)

A desktop app runs Telegram in its own window and translates the conversation as it happens. With GlotChat:

  1. Install GlotChat on Windows 10 or 11 and add your Telegram account (log in or scan the QR code).
  2. Pick your language once. Incoming messages appear translated under the original.
  3. Type your reply in your own language — before it sends, GlotChat shows what the other person will read, checks every number, and offers other wordings.
  4. Voice notes and photo text are translated too, and translation runs on your own PC, so your chats aren't sent to a cloud service.

Good for: real conversations, customers who write in other languages, and anyone who also uses WhatsApp — GlotChat runs WhatsApp accounts in the same window. It translates 49 languages offline.

A note on privacy

Copy-paste and bots send your text to someone else's server. For personal, business or financial chats that matters. An app that translates offline on your computer keeps the conversation on your machine — only turn on an online engine if you choose to.

Tips for Telegram in another language

  • Check before you send. A wrong number or name in a translated reply can cost a sale — GlotChat shows the back-translation and flags missing numbers.
  • Reply in their language. It's the fastest way to earn trust, and with translation built in it takes no extra time.
  • Keep accounts separate. If you run several Telegram numbers, give each a clear name so you always know which one you're answering from.
