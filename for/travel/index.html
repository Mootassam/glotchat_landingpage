FAQ

Travel questions

Do guests notice that messages are translated?

No. They just receive a message in their language.

Can our team use it in English while guests write in other languages?

Yes. Each agent reads translations in their own language and replies in it; guests receive their language.

What do we need to start?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and the phones of the WhatsApp numbers you want to add (to scan a QR code once). The free trial takes about 10 minutes to set up.
