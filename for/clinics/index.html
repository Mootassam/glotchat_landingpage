FAQ

Clinic questions

Is translation accurate enough for medical topics?

It's reliable for everyday patient communication — questions, scheduling, prices, travel. For diagnoses, consent forms or medical instructions, have the final wording checked by a qualified person.

Are patient messages sent to any server?

No. Translation, voice transcription and photo reading run on your own PC. WhatsApp itself works as usual.

Can we use it on several computers?

Business includes up to 3 computers. Larger teams can ask us for a custom offer.
