FAQ

Multiple WhatsApp accounts — questions

How many WhatsApp accounts can I run on one PC?

We've tested 27 at the same time. The real limit is memory: each account uses about 300–450 MB, so 8 GB RAM handles about 10 accounts and 16 GB or more handles 20–30.

Do I need a phone for each account?

Yes. Every WhatsApp account belongs to a phone number, and you link it once by scanning a QR code with that phone. The phone doesn't have to stay next to the PC.

Can each account use its own proxy?

Yes. Open the account's settings and paste a proxy (http:// or socks5://, with username and password if needed). GlotChat checks it and shows an alert if it stops working.

Will my accounts get banned?

GlotChat uses the official WhatsApp Web, so it's the same as using WhatsApp in a browser. Bans come from behaviour WhatsApp forbids — spam, bulk unsolicited messages, modified apps. Use your accounts normally and you're fine.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes — WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts can both be linked.
