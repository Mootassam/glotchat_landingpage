Billing questions

Paying, renewing and upgrading

How does the free trial work?

Download and install GlotChat, then click “Start my free 3-day trial” in the app. Your 3-day trial starts at once with every feature unlocked. No payment details are needed, and there is one free trial per computer.

How do I pay?

In USDT on TRC20, BEP20 or ERC20. We send you the payment address on Telegram, and your yearly key as soon as the payment arrives.

Is the price per account or per computer?

Per plan. Each plan includes a number of WhatsApp accounts and computers (Starter and Pro: 1 computer, Business: up to 3).

What happens after one year?

You renew at the same price and keep working without interruption. Your accounts, chats and settings stay on your PC the whole time.

Can I upgrade or add computers?

Yes, at any time. You only pay the difference for the rest of your year. For more computers or a lifetime license, ask for a custom offer.

Do I get updates?

Yes. All updates released during your year are included and install automatically.
