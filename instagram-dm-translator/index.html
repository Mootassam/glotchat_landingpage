FAQ

Instagram DM translator — questions

Can I translate Instagram DMs on PC?

Yes. In GlotChat every Instagram Direct message is translated automatically under the original, in every conversation of every Instagram account you add.

Does it translate my replies?

Yes. Write in your language and press Enter: GlotChat shows the translation and what the customer will read before you send.

Can I use several Instagram accounts at the same time?

Yes. Each account has its own isolated session, so all inboxes are open side by side — as many as your plan includes.

Are voice messages and photos translated on Instagram?

Not yet: on Instagram, text messages are translated both ways. Voice notes and photos are translated on WhatsApp and Telegram.

Is it safe for my Instagram account?

GlotChat opens the official Instagram website, exactly like a browser, and never sends anything by itself. As always, follow Instagram's rules — no spam or bulk messages.
