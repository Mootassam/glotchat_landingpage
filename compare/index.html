FAQ

Comparison questions

Is GlotChat better than a WhatsApp translator extension?

For one account and occasional translation, an extension may be enough. GlotChat adds many accounts in one window, voice-note and photo translation, translation of your replies, and it translates offline instead of sending your chats to a web service.

Why pay when WhatsApp Web is free?

WhatsApp Web shows one account per browser session and doesn't translate. When you run many numbers and talk to customers in other languages, the time lost switching and copy-pasting quickly costs more than a GlotChat plan.
