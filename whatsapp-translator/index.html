How it works

Three things happen in every chat

  1. 1

    They write in their language

    Each incoming message shows its translation right under the original — automatically, as it arrives.

  2. 2

    You write in yours

    Type your reply in your own language. GlotChat shows the translation so you can check it before sending.

  3. 3

    They receive theirs

    The customer gets natural text in their language. In your chat you still see what you wrote.

FAQ

WhatsApp translator questions

Can I translate WhatsApp messages automatically on PC?

Yes. GlotChat translates every incoming message automatically and shows the translation under the original, in every chat and on every account you add.

Does the customer see that I use a translator?

No. They simply receive your message in their language. Nothing is added to the conversation.

Can it translate WhatsApp voice messages?

Yes. Click Transcribe under a voice note: GlotChat turns it into text and translates it, offline. About 100 spoken languages are recognised.

How good is the translation?

Good for everyday business conversation — prices, orders, appointments, questions. For contracts or legal wording, always have important texts checked by a person.

Do I need an internet connection to translate?

Only once, to download each language pack. After that translation works offline on your PC.
