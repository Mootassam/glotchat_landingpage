How it works

Three things happen in every chat

  1. 1

    They write in their language

    Each incoming message shows its translation right under the original — automatically, as it arrives.

  2. 2

    You write in yours

    Type your reply in your own language and press Enter. GlotChat shows the translation and what the customer will read.

  3. 3

    They receive theirs

    The customer gets natural text in their language. In your chat you still see what you wrote.

Check before you send

Never send a translation you can't read

Press Enter and GlotChat shows the translation in a small preview. Under it, the same text translated back into your language. If a number is missing, you get a clear warning — and in Auto mode, the message waits for you instead of going out.

  • “They will read” updates as you edit the translation
  • “Other wording” offers a few ways to say it

Offline & private

Your customer chats never leave your computer

Online translators and most browser extensions send the text you translate to their servers. GlotChat uses translation models that run on your own PC — after a one-time download per language, it works without sending a single message anywhere.

  • No translation subscription, no per-character fees
  • Works even if your internet is slow — only WhatsApp itself needs it
  • Safer for medical, legal and financial conversations

FAQ

WhatsApp translator questions

Can I translate WhatsApp messages automatically on PC?

Yes. GlotChat translates every incoming message automatically and shows the translation under the original, in every chat and on every account you add.

Does the customer see that I use a translator?

No. They simply receive your message in their language. Nothing is added to the conversation.

How do I know the translation is right?

Before sending, GlotChat shows your translation translated back into your language (“They will read”). It also checks every price, date and phone number, and offers other wordings if you want a different one.

Can it translate WhatsApp voice messages?

Yes. Click Transcribe under a voice note: GlotChat turns it into text and translates it, offline. About 100 spoken languages are recognised, and long voice notes show their progress.

Do I need an internet connection to translate?

Only once, to download each language pack. After that translation works offline on your PC.

Does it translate Telegram too?

Yes — the same translation works in Telegram accounts. See the Telegram translator page.
