3. Copy your Machine ID

In GlotChat, open Settings → License and click Copy next to your Machine ID. Your key will be made for this computer.

4. Message us on Telegram

Write to @GlotChat with your plan and your Machine ID. We reply with the exact amount and our USDT address — only trust an address you get from @GlotChat.

5. Get USDT (if you don't have any)

USDT is a digital dollar: 1 USDT ≈ 1 US dollar. The easiest ways to get it:

  • Binance or OKX: create an account, then buy USDT with your card, a bank transfer or P2P (you pay a local seller in your own currency — cash apps, bank, mobile money).
  • Bybit, KuCoin, Bitget and most other exchanges work the same way.
  • A friend or colleague who already has USDT can pay for you — that's fine.

6. Send the USDT — on the right network

USDT exists on several networks. We accept TRC20 (Tron), BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain) and ERC20 (Ethereum). The network you choose when sending must match the address we gave you.

  1. In your exchange, go to Withdraw → USDT.
  2. Paste our address and pick the same network we named (TRC20 or BEP20 usually have the lowest fees; ERC20 can be expensive).
  3. Check that the amount we receive is the full plan price — the exchange fee comes on top.
  4. Confirm. Then send us the screenshot or the transaction ID (TXID) on Telegram.

7. Paste your key

Your yearly key usually arrives within minutes. In GlotChat, go to Settings → Enter a new key and paste it. Your accounts, logins and settings stay exactly as they were in the trial.

Safety tips

  • Only send to an address you received from @GlotChat on Telegram. We never ask for your exchange password, seed phrase or codes.
  • Double-check the network. USDT sent on the wrong network can be lost.
  • Keep the TXID until your key works.

TRC20 or BEP20 — they usually have the lowest fees. ERC20 also works but can cost several dollars in fees. Always use the network we name with the address.

Usually within minutes after the payment arrives. A real person handles every order on Telegram.

Can someone else pay for me?

Yes. A friend, colleague or your company can send the USDT — just tell us on Telegram which payment is yours.
