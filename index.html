FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Message us on Telegram and you get a 3-day trial key with every feature unlocked — no payment needed. If you like it, you buy a plan and keep all your accounts and settings.

Is it safe for my WhatsApp accounts?

GlotChat opens the official WhatsApp Web for each account, in its own isolated session — exactly like a browser, just organised. It never sends messages by itself: everything is sent by you. As with any WhatsApp use, avoid spam and bulk unsolicited messages.

Does translation need the internet?

No. Translation, voice transcription and photo reading run on your own PC. You download a language pack once (English is built in). WhatsApp itself of course needs the internet.

Which computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Each WhatsApp account uses about 300–450 MB of memory: 8 GB RAM is fine for about 10 accounts, 16 GB or more for 20–30 accounts.

Which languages are supported?

49 translation languages, including English, Chinese, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese. Voice messages are recognised in about 100 languages.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts can be linked, the same way you link WhatsApp Web.

Do I need to scan a QR code for every account?

Yes, once per account — with WhatsApp on that phone → Linked devices. After that the account stays logged in, even after restarting the PC.

How do I pay and receive the software?

Message us on Telegram, choose your plan and pay in USDT (TRC20, BEP20 or ERC20). You receive the installer and your license key, and we help you set it up if needed.

What happens after one year?

You renew your plan at the same price and keep working without interruption. Your accounts, chats and settings stay on your PC.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. When you add more WhatsApp numbers, message us and we upgrade your key — you only pay the difference.

Does it work on Mac or phone?

GlotChat is a Windows desktop app. Your phones keep working normally — GlotChat is a linked device, like WhatsApp Web.

Can my Chinese-speaking team use it?

Yes. The whole interface is available in Chinese, and agents can read and write in Chinese while customers receive their own language.
