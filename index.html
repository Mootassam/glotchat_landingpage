FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it safe for my WhatsApp accounts?

GlotChat opens the official WhatsApp Web for each account, in its own isolated session — exactly like a browser, just organised. It never sends messages by itself: everything is sent by you.

Does translation need the internet?

No. Translation, voice transcription and photo reading run on your own PC. You only download a language pack once (English is built in). WhatsApp itself of course needs the internet.

Which computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Each WhatsApp account uses about 300–450 MB of memory: 8 GB RAM is fine for about 10 accounts, 16 GB or more for 20–30 accounts.

Which languages are supported?

49 translation languages, including English, Chinese, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese. Voice messages are recognised in about 100 languages.

How do I pay and receive the software?

Message us on Telegram, choose your plan and pay in USDT. You receive the installer, and we help you set it up if needed.

Do I need to scan a QR code for every account?

Yes, once per account — with WhatsApp on that phone → Linked devices. After that the account stays logged in, even after restarting the PC.

Does it work on Mac or phone?

GlotChat is a Windows desktop app. Your phones keep working normally — GlotChat is a linked device, like WhatsApp Web.

Can my Chinese-speaking team use it?

Yes. The whole interface is available in Chinese, and agents can read and write in Chinese while customers receive their own language.
