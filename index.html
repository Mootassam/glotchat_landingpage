FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download GlotChat, open it and click “Start my free 3-day trial” — every feature is unlocked at once, no payment needed. If you like it, you buy a plan and keep all your accounts and settings.

Which apps does it work with?

WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct and Discord — several accounts of each, in one window. Chats are translated both ways in every app; voice notes and photos are translated on WhatsApp and Telegram.

Is it safe for my accounts?

GlotChat opens the official website of each app (WhatsApp Web, Telegram Web, Messenger, Instagram, Discord) for each account, in its own isolated session — exactly like a browser, just organised. It never sends messages by itself: everything is sent by you. As with any messenger, avoid spam and bulk unsolicited messages.

How good is the translation?

Very good for everyday business chat — prices, orders, appointments, questions. And you never have to guess: before you send, GlotChat shows what the customer will read, checks every number, and offers other wordings. For contracts or medical wording, have important texts checked by a person.

Does translation need the internet?

No. Translation, voice transcription and photo reading run on your own PC. You download a language pack once (English is built in). WhatsApp and Telegram themselves of course need the internet.

Which computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Each account uses about 300–450 MB of memory: 8 GB RAM is fine for about 10 accounts, 16 GB or more for 20–30.

How do I pay and receive the software?

Download the free trial from this website. When you're ready, message us on Telegram, choose your plan and pay in USDT (TRC20, BEP20 or ERC20). Your license key arrives within minutes, and we help you set up if needed.

Does it work on Mac or phone?

GlotChat is a Windows desktop app. Your phones keep working normally — GlotChat is a linked device, like WhatsApp Web or Telegram Desktop.
