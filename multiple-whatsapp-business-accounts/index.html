FAQ

WhatsApp Business questions

Can I use multiple WhatsApp Business accounts on one PC?

Yes. In GlotChat each WhatsApp Business number runs in its own isolated session, side by side in one window — up to 5 on Starter, 15 on Pro, unlimited on Business.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

No. GlotChat uses the normal WhatsApp Business app on your phone, linked like WhatsApp Web. No Meta approval, no per-message fees.

Do labels and the catalog still work?

Yes. GlotChat shows the official WhatsApp Web, so the Business features available there work as usual.

Can I mix normal WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Both count as WhatsApp accounts in your plan and run side by side.
