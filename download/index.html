Download FAQ

Questions about downloading and installing

Is GlotChat free to download?

Yes. The download is free and the 3-day trial unlocks every feature with no payment. After the trial, plans start at 69 USDT a year.

Why does Windows say “Windows protected your PC”?

Windows SmartScreen shows this warning for new apps it hasn't seen many times yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Always download GlotChat from this page — you can compare the SHA-256 checksum to be sure the file is the original.

Why is the installer version older than the newest GlotChat?

The installer stays on a version Windows already trusts, so it installs smoothly everywhere. A minute after you start GlotChat, it updates itself to the newest version (with Telegram and every new feature).

Do I need a license key?

Not for the trial: click Start my free 3-day trial in the app and it starts at once. When you buy a plan, we send you a yearly key on Telegram — paste it under I already have a license key.

Is there a version for Mac, Android or iPhone?

Not at the moment. GlotChat is a Windows 10 and 11 desktop app. Your accounts stay on your phones — GlotChat links them to your PC like WhatsApp Web does.

How do I uninstall GlotChat?

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find GlotChat and click Uninstall.

The download stopped or is slow. What can I do?

Click the download button again. If it still doesn't work, message us on Telegram and we send you the installer directly.
